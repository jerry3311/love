# 🌹 Angela's Golden Rose

Welcome to a little corner of the internet made entirely for you, Angela. 

I built this website as a digital love letter—a rose that will never wilt, and a place you can always visit to remember how much you mean to me. 

### ✨ What's Inside?
* **A 3D Golden Rose:** A custom-coded, slowly rotating rose made of gold that reacts to your mouse.
* **Falling Petals:** A button to shower you in gold petals whenever you want.
* **Our Time:** A live counter showing exactly how many days, hours, and minutes we've been together. *(You can set our special date on the site!)*
* **A Hidden Letter:** Tap my name 5 times on the website to unlock a secret message.
* **A Song:** A built-in music player so you can play our song while you read.
